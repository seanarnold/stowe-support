Common questions

How do I label a box?

Tap New container. Stowe creates a numbered QR label. Choose Print label, Share or Save to Photos (a square image with the QR code, number and name). Stick the label on the side you see first when the box is shelved. You can reprint it later from the container's QR button.

How do I find an item?

Use the Find tab to search every container at once. Each result shows which box the item is in. You can also tap the scan button in the middle of the tab bar and point the camera at a label to see what's inside.

How do I move items between boxes?

Open a container, choose Move items, then pick the items and a destination. You can move part of a quantity, and you can undo a move straight afterwards.

What does Stowe Pro include?

Stowe is free for up to 2 containers. Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited containers, AR view (see what's inside each labelled box through the camera) and Add from photo (suggests items from a photo, recognised on your device). It's a lifetime upgrade, one payment with every future feature included.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked

Open any Pro prompt and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought with. If it still doesn't unlock, email me and include your order details from Apple.

Does Stowe sync between my devices?

Not yet. Your containers and items are stored on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes its data, so keep that in mind before removing it or switching phones.

Why does Stowe want camera and Photos access?

The camera is used to scan labels and to photograph items. Photos access is add-only, and is used only when you tap Save to Photos. Stowe can't read your photo library.
