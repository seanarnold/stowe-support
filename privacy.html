Privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Stowe is built to keep your data on your phone. In short: I don't collect, send or sell any of your data.

What Stowe stores

The containers, items, quantities and photos you add are stored only on your iPhone. They aren't uploaded to any server, and I can't see them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Camera

Stowe uses the camera to scan container labels, to show AR view, and to photograph items. Images from the camera are processed on your device. A photo you attach to an item is saved inside the app with that item.

Photo recognition

The Pro feature Add from photo suggests items from a photo using Apple's on-device Vision framework. The photo is not sent anywhere.

Photos

When you choose Save to Photos, Stowe asks for add-only access so it can save a label image. It can't read or change the rest of your library. When you pick an existing photo for an item, you choose it with the system photo picker and Stowe sees only that photo.

Purchases

Stowe Pro is an in-app purchase processed by Apple. I never see your payment details or Apple Account. Stowe checks your purchase with Apple only to unlock Pro.

What I don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data

Children

Stowe doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date above.
